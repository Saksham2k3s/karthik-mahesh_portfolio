Portfolio content & structure (living reference)

SITE CONFIG (`src/config/site.ts`)
- Update name, title, location, contact links, summary, and highlights.
- Replace `portrait` with your photo at `public/image.png`.

DESIGN DIRECTION
- Cinematic image-led split layout (not editorial paper style).
- Desktop: fixed left portrait column; content scrolls on the right.
- Mobile: full-bleed portrait hero, dark content bands below.
- Aboreto + Outfit typography, charcoal + gold palette.

CORE SECTIONS
- Home — hero with profile image, summary, CTAs, stats.
- Experience — timeline cards.
- Projects — case study panel.
- Skills — categorized tags.
- Education — degree cards.
- Certificates — credential grid.
- Contact — split panel with portrait visual.

DEPLOYMENT
- Netlify: `npm run build`, publish `dist`.
